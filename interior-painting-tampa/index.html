Common Questions

Interior Painting FAQ

How much does interior painting cost in Tampa?
Most single rooms in the Tampa area run between $400 and $900, depending on room size, ceiling height, and how much prep work the walls need. Whole-home interiors typically range from $3,500 to $8,000+. We give you an exact, fixed quote after a free walk-through — and the price you approve is the price you pay.
How many coats of paint do you apply?
Two coats is our standard for a rich, even finish. If you're making a dramatic color change or painting over stains or repairs, we add a primer coat first. We never thin paint to stretch it — what we quote is what goes on your walls.
Do you provide color consultation?
Yes. Choosing colors is one of the hardest parts of any painting project. We help you narrow down options, provide sample swatches, and can paint test patches on your walls — so you can see the color in your own light before committing.
How long does it take to paint a room?
A standard bedroom or living room usually takes one day, including prep and two coats. A whole-home interior typically takes 3 to 5 days depending on size and how much trim and ceiling work is included. You get a clear timeline before we start.
Do I need to move furniture before painters arrive?
No. We move and cover your furniture, protect your floors, and put everything back when we're done. The only thing we ask: please remove small valuables and fragile items from the room.
What paint brands do you use?
We work with premium lines from Sherwin-Williams and Benjamin Moore — paints that cover better, last longer, and stand up to Florida's humidity. If you have a preferred brand or product, we're happy to use it.
